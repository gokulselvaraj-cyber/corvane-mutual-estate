# ADR-0011: Fraud referral threshold

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2024-11-18
- **Deciders:** Platform architecture board

## Decision

A claim is referred to the fraud team when its fraud score is **0.80 or higher**. Below that, triage continues normally.

## Consequences

About 4% of claims are referred. The threshold is reviewed every six months by the fraud analytics team.
