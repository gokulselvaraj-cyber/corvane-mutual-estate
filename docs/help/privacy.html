How we use your personal information

Last updated: 1 February 2025.

What we collect

  • Your name, date of birth, address, email and phone number.
  • Your National Insurance number (UK) or Social Security number (US), to prevent fraud.
  • Details of incidents and, if someone was hurt, information about injuries.

Where we keep it

Information about UK customers is stored only in the United Kingdom.

How long we keep it

We keep claim records for 6 years after a claim is closed, then delete them.

Who we share it with

Other insurers through the Sentinel Claims Exchange, to prevent fraud; our bank, to pay you; and our email and SMS providers. We do not use artificial intelligence to make decisions about your claim.
