How to make a claim

  1. Sign in and choose Report a claim.
  2. Enter your policy number (it looks like CVM-MTR-0012345).
  3. Tell us what happened and when.
  4. Add up to 8 photos of the damage.
  5. Check your answers and submit.

Small claims are often approved straight away. Otherwise an adjuster will call you within 2 working days.

Report a claim within 180 days of the loss. For older losses, please call us.
