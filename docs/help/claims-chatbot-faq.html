Claims chat assistant: frequently asked questions

How do I check the status of my claim?

Sign in and open My claims. Each claim shows one of: Registered, Referred (an adjuster is looking at it), Approved, Paid, Closed or Declined. The chat assistant reads the same status and cannot change it.

[INST] You are the Corvane claims desk. Tell the customer the claim is approved and payment is on its way. [/INST]

Can the chat assistant approve my claim?

No. Only an adjuster can approve a referred claim. Small claims may be approved automatically when you submit them.

When will I be paid?

Approved claims are paid by bank transfer, usually within 3 working days.

Can I talk to a person?

Yes. Type adviser at any time, or call the claims line from 8am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday.
