# Travel (TRV): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 10,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 150, 200, 500.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Single trip or annual | Sets the sum insured |
| Destination | Sets the sum insured |
| Number of travellers | Underwriting acceptance |
| Age of the oldest traveller | Underwriting acceptance |
| Any pre-existing medical conditions? | Underwriting acceptance |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *destination* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Travel against government advice
- Winter sports unless added
- Undeclared medical conditions

## Optional extras

- Winter sports
- Gadget cover abroad

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Travel claims are handled by the
personal lines claims team.
