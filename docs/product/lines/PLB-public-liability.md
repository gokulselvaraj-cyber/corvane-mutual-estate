# Public liability (PLB): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 10,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 150, 200, 750.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Trade or business | Fraud prevention |
| Annual turnover | Fraud prevention |
| Number of employees | Underwriting acceptance |
| Limit of indemnity | Fraud prevention |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *annualTurnover* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Professional advice
- Work at heights over 15m unless declared

## Optional extras

- Employers' liability
- Product liability

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Public liability claims are handled by the
specialty claims team.
