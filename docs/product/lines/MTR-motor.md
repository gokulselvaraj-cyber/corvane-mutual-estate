# Motor (MTR): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 150,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 200, 400, 500, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Vehicle registration | Loading or discount |
| Vehicle value | Fraud prevention |
| Annual mileage | Loading or discount |
| Main driver age | Main rating factor |
| Where is it kept overnight? | Loading or discount |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *annualMileage* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Racing or track days
- Wear and tear
- Driving without a valid licence

## Optional extras

- Breakdown cover
- Courtesy car
- Legal expenses

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Motor claims are handled by the
personal lines claims team.
