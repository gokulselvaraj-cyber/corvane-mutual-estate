# Home buildings (HOM): product guide

**Sold in:** UK. **Maximum cover:** 2,500,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 250, 300, 750.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Rebuild cost | Loading or discount |
| Year built | Loading or discount |
| Roof type | Sets the sum insured |
| Is it a listed building? | Loading or discount |
| Has the property flooded in the last 10 years? | Fraud prevention |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *yearBuilt* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Gradual deterioration
- Subsidence in the first 12 months
- Unoccupied over 60 days

## Optional extras

- Accidental damage
- Home emergency

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Home buildings claims are handled by the
specialty claims team.
