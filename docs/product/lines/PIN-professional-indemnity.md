# Professional indemnity (PIN): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 5,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 250, 400, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Profession | Sets the sum insured |
| Annual fee income | Sets the sum insured |
| Claims in the last 5 years | Main rating factor |
| Limit of indemnity | Main rating factor |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *claimsLast5Years* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Known circumstances before inception
- Fines and penalties

## Optional extras

- Run-off cover
- Defamation

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Professional indemnity claims are handled by the
specialty claims team.
