# Gadget (GDG): product guide

**Sold in:** UK. **Maximum cover:** 4,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 100, 400, 500.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Device type | Sets the sum insured |
| Purchase price | Sets the sum insured |
| Purchase date | Underwriting acceptance |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *purchaseDate* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Cosmetic damage
- Unattended in public
- Devices over 18 months old at purchase

## Optional extras

- Worldwide cover
- Unauthorised call cover

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Gadget claims are handled by the
specialty claims team.
