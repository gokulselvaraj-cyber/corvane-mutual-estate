# Landlord (LLD): product guide

**Sold in:** UK. **Maximum cover:** 2,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 200, 300, 500, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Number of let properties | Sets the sum insured |
| Tenant type | Main rating factor |
| Rent per month | Underwriting acceptance |
| House in multiple occupation? | Loading or discount |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *propertiesCount* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Malicious damage by tenants unless added
- Unoccupied over 30 days

## Optional extras

- Rent guarantee
- Malicious damage by tenants

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Landlord claims are handled by the
commercial lines claims team.
