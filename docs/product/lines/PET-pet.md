# Pet (PET): product guide

**Sold in:** UK. **Maximum cover:** 15,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 200, 400, 750, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Species | Main rating factor |
| Breed | Underwriting acceptance |
| Age in years | Loading or discount |
| Microchipped? | Main rating factor |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *microchipped* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Pre-existing conditions
- Routine vaccinations
- Pregnancy

## Optional extras

- Lifetime cover
- Overseas travel

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Pet claims are handled by the
personal lines claims team.
