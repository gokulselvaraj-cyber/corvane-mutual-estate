# Cyber (CYB): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 10,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 250, 750, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Annual revenue | Main rating factor |
| Personal data records held | Loading or discount |
| Is MFA enforced for all remote access? | Main rating factor |
| Are backups kept offline? | Loading or discount |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *annualRevenue* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Unpatched critical vulnerabilities over 30 days
- War and state-backed attacks
- Infrastructure outages

## Optional extras

- Ransom negotiation
- Social engineering fraud

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Cyber claims are handled by the
commercial lines claims team.
