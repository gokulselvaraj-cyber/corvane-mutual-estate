# Marine cargo (MAR): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 5,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 200, 250, 400, 500.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Cargo type | Main rating factor |
| Annual shipped value | Sets the sum insured |
| Main conveyance | Loading or discount |
| Usual Incoterm | Main rating factor |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *incoterm* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Inherent vice
- Insufficient packing
- Delay

## Optional extras

- War and strikes
- Storage up to 60 days

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Marine cargo claims are handled by the
commercial lines claims team.
