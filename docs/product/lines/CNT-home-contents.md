# Home contents (CNT): product guide

**Sold in:** UK. **Maximum cover:** 250,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 0, 200, 250, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Total contents value | Sets the sum insured |
| Jewellery, watches and art | Sets the sum insured |
| Front door lock | Main rating factor |
| Burglar alarm fitted? | Sets the sum insured |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *alarm* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Items left in an unlocked car
- Money over £500
- Pets damaging contents

## Optional extras

- Personal possessions away from home
- Bicycle cover

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Home contents claims are handled by the
personal lines claims team.
