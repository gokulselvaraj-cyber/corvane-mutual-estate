# Commercial property (CPR): product guide

**Sold in:** UK and US. **Maximum cover:** 50,000,000. **Voluntary excess options:** 200, 400, 500, 1000.

## What we ask at quote

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Occupancy | Main rating factor |
| Buildings sum insured | Main rating factor |
| Sprinklers fitted? | Main rating factor |
| Business interruption period (months) | Sets the sum insured |

Quotes are referred to an underwriter when *buildingSumInsured* is outside appetite.

## Not covered

- Flood in high-risk zones unless agreed
- Cyber events

## Optional extras

- Business interruption
- Terrorism

## Claims

Report a claim online through the five-step claim form, or by phone. Commercial property claims are handled by the
specialty claims team.
